# Inbox with assistance

The default Inbox helps Muness find meaningful email, reduce recurring noise, and teach standing preferences. It combines personal correspondence, actionable suggestions, source evidence, and free-text instructions in the same daily workflow.

## Accepted behavior

- Prioritize meaningful mail while keeping all accounts and archived mail searchable.
- Offer unsubscribe for specific mailing lists and narrow ongoing rules for repetitive purchases.
- Preserve receipt candidates independently of inbox state and distinguish consulting from AlixPartners reimbursement.
- Interpret paragraphs into selectable one-off actions and standing rules. Preserve ambiguity explicitly and support corrections.
- Keep provider actions account-scoped and show completion or failure in Activity.
- Support mobile, keyboard selection/navigation, source threads, and existing compose.

## Constraints

Real mail only. NAS-backed storage and runtime, 9950X for compute. Existing Tailnet access. Preserve the incumbent visual language with a restrained task-oriented interface. Mail content supplies evidence, never authority for automations.

## Success

The user can unsubscribe from named newsletters, define a purchase-only archive rule, route a receipt to its business, find archived receipts, revise a rule, and inspect provider outcome without leaving the mail workflow.
